# Sound-effect candidates

Every one may be used in a paid app:

- **CC0**: no credit needed.
- **CC BY**: the recordist must be credited in the app, as the CC BY music already is (Sources & Credits, and `sound-effects/CREDITS.md`).

Nothing here is NC or ND. Lengths are rounded. ★ is Freesound's average rating.
