The opening, the surround and the pierced holes in the carving were made transparent, and a grey rim trimmed from the outer edge. Slivers of the grey wall left along the right side (rows 889 to 1064 of the upright file) were removed on 13 October 2026, and a fine outline in dark brown was drawn round the outside of the carving and its pierced holes, not round the opening; on 21 October 2026 it was redrawn thinner and darker (#26160a, about 2.5 pixels of the file). The pierced holes in the carving (47 of them, on the sides, crests and corners) were filled with a deep shadow brown (#2e1d0f) so that the wall does not show through them as pale spots, and grey left along the rim was repainted brown. The file has 8 clear pixels on every side so that the outline is not cut off.
