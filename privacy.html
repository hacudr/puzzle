Privacy Policy

Art History, picture puzzles of great paintings · effective 25 October 2026

Art History collects no personal data. It has no account to sign up for, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing about you to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

To keep your place, the app remembers some things on your iPad or iPhone:

  • which puzzles you have solved, and the pieces of any puzzle left half done;
  • your settings, such as the difficulty, the sound and the frame;
  • which collections you have unlocked.

These stay in the app's own storage on the device. We never receive them. They are deleted when you delete the app, and they may be included in your device's own backups, as with any app.

Purchases

Collections are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and we never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. When it starts, and when you tap Restore purchases, the app asks the App Store which collections your Apple Account owns, so that they unlock. Apple's own privacy policy covers the purchase itself.

The internet

The pictures, music and texts are all inside the app, so it plays without a connection. The only time it goes online is to reach the App Store for purchases, as described above.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included. Its Kids mode hides pictures with nudity, violence or frightening subjects.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins to collect information, this page will say so first, with a new date at the top.
